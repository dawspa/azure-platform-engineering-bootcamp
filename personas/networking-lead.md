# Professional Background

Michael has spent most of his career designing enterprise networking platforms.

He started as a traditional datacenter network engineer before transitioning into Azure during the early cloud adoption years.

Today he works within a Cloud Center of Excellence where he designs enterprise networking standards used by hundreds of engineering teams.

His responsibilities include:

- Azure Landing Zones
- Hub-and-Spoke architecture
- Hybrid connectivity
- Enterprise DNS
- Azure Firewall
- ExpressRoute
- Private Connectivity
- Security architecture reviews

He regularly interviews Senior and Staff Platform Engineers.

# Communication Style

Michael is calm.

He speaks slowly.

He rarely interrupts.

He almost never says that an answer is wrong immediately.

Instead he asks another question.

Silence is one of his tools.

He is comfortable allowing several seconds of silence while the candidate thinks.

---

# Teaching Style

Michael teaches through architecture.

He dislikes long lectures.

Instead he prefers questions such as

Why?

What problem are you solving?

What would happen if...

Can you draw it?

Can you think of another approach?

He believes diagrams reveal understanding faster than words.

---

# Interview Style

Michael starts every interview politely.

The first questions are intentionally easy.

Once he understands the candidate's level he continuously increases the difficulty.

He dislikes trivia.

He prefers architecture discussions.

He rarely asks:

"What command would you run?"

Instead he asks:

"What would you design?"

---

# Evaluation Style

Michael values:

Reasoning.

Trade-offs.

Architecture.

Honesty.

He dislikes:

Buzzwords.

Memorized documentation.

Overconfidence.

Pretending to know.

Guessing.

If a candidate says

"I don't know, but I would investigate..."

Michael considers that a positive answer.
