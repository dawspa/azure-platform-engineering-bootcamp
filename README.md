# Azure Platform Engineering Interview Bootcamp

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This repository contains an AI-native interview preparation framework for experienced Platform Engineers preparing for enterprise Azure interviews.

It is not a certification guide.

It is not a collection of notes.

It is a structured learning system focused on architectural thinking, technical communication and interview readiness.

---

# Design Philosophy

The framework separates the learning engine from the learning content.

```
Framework

↓

Content Packs

↓

AI Sessions

↓

Interview Readiness
```

The framework should remain stable.

Only the content packs evolve.

# First Learning Session

This framework is designed to work with any capable AI assistant.

Recommended workflow

---

## Step 1

Open today's module specification.

---

## Step 2

Open the following files in your editor.

framework/module-template.md

framework/prompt-standard.md

Selected persona

Today's module specification

---

## Step 3

Open a new AI conversation.

Attach or paste the files above.

Ask the AI assistant:

"Generate a complete learning module according to the attached specification."

Save the generated output as

generated.md

---

## Step 4

Open another AI conversation.

Copy the Teacher Prompt from generated.md.

Complete the learning session.

---

## Step 5

Open another AI conversation.

Copy the Coach Prompt.

Complete the discussion.

---

## Step 6

Open another AI conversation.

Copy the Interview Prompt.

At the end of the interview ask the AI to generate the Interview Report.

Save it.

---

## Step 7

Open another AI conversation.

Paste the Interview Report.

Copy the Reviewer Prompt.

Generate the Evaluation Report.

---

## Step 8

If Critical or Major gaps exist

perform Gap Closure

followed by

Validation Interview.

---

## Step 9

When Validation succeeds

mark the module as completed.

# Daily Routine

Complete one module.

Finish the workflow.

Stop.

Do not continue simply because time remains.

Consistency is more valuable than marathon study sessions.

---

# Completion Rules

A module is complete only when:

- Exit Criteria are satisfied
- Validation Interview succeeds
- No Critical gaps remain
- No Major gaps remain

Minor gaps are acceptable.

Interview readiness matters more than perfection.
